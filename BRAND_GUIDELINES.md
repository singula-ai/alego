# Alego Brand Asset Usage Guidelines

English | [中文](BRAND_GUIDELINES.zh.md)

To maintain the long\-term healthy development of the Alego ecosystem, avoid user confusion, and facilitate the retrieval and identification of related resources, we have established these specifications and hope that everyone will adhere to them:

- In your project's descriptive text, you may use "Alego" to truthfully and accurately describe your project's relationship with Alego, for example, "built on Alego" or "compatible with Alego\." Such descriptions comply with license requirements and help users understand your project's positioning\.
- If you wish your project name to reflect its association with the Alego ecosystem, we recommend a name of the form "Alego \<your project\>" or "\<your project\> for Alego" rather than one that reads as an official release\.
- When naming your project, please avoid presenting "Alego" as the project's own name\. Unauthorized use in project names can easily lead to user misunderstanding and confusion, thereby affecting the clarity of the entire ecosystem\.
- Additionally, please avoid using official brand materials in your promotions or presentations in a way that could cause misunderstanding, so as not to give users the false impression of official endorsement, cooperation, or authorization\.

## Upstream marks

Alego is a fork of [DeepSeek Harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness)\. "DeepSeek" and "DeepSeek Harness" are marks of DeepSeek, and Alego claims no rights in them\. Alego ships none of DeepSeek's logos, wordmarks, or badge artwork; those were replaced during the rebrand\. Where the code names DeepSeek — the `llm-deepseek` provider, the `deepseek-official` provider route, `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY`, `api.deepseek.com`, and the DeepSeek model ids — it does so to integrate with the DeepSeek API as a third\-party service, which is nominative use and not a claim of affiliation\.

We believe that a clear and orderly community environment will make every developer's efforts more visible and more readily recognized\. For the few cases that do not comply with the above specifications, we may contact the relevant parties to make appropriate adjustments in order to maintain the overall order of the ecosystem\. Thank you for your understanding and support—let us work together to build a more friendly and sustainable open\-source community\.
